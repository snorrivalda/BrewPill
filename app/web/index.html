1 · Capsule axis

Pick the board axis that runs along the length of your capsule. Tip the capsule from upright to lying flat: the right axis goes from about 0° to about 90°.

2 · Calibration points

Float the pill in water and in 2–5 sugar solutions whose gravity you've measured with a hydrometer (SG) or refractometer (°Brix). Wait until the tilt settles (about 5 minutes; readings arrive once a minute), then add a point. To add a point by hand, type its tilt too. Edit any tilt or measurement in the table, and untick a point to leave it out of the fit.

3 · Temperature

The pill uses the nRF51's on-chip sensor, which can be a few degrees off. Enter a thermometer reading taken at the same time to correct it.

4 · Send to gateway

The pill only broadcasts raw readings. The BrewPill gateway turns them into gravity for Brewfather and webhooks, so send it the axis, curve and temperature offset above. The gateway and this Mac must be on the same network.
